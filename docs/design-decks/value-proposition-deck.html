Why this matters

The current pain

Most independent gyms run on a mix of paper, spreadsheets, and memory — and members don't have anywhere that feels like "theirs."

Notebooks at the front desk
Attendance and sign-ins tracked by hand
Scattered payment receipts
Cash and GCash, no single record
Manual attendance
No live view of who's actually in the gym
No single member home
Nowhere for members to feel part of something
Stren03 / 14
Why Stren

Help gyms feel organized, distinct, and easy to return to

Stren isn't trying to replace the gym floor — it's trying to make the parts around it (identity, ops, belonging) feel less chaotic.

Organized
One place for members, plans, payments, and check-ins — no more notebooks.
Distinct
A branded gym page — your logo, your colors, your story, not a generic listing.
A reason to return
Streaks, feed, and leaderboard turn showing up into something visible.

Being honest about scope

What Stren is not yet

We'd rather tell you what's missing than let a demo imply otherwise.

Routine builder
Not a Hevy/Lyfta-style workout planner
Friends / social graph
No follow or friend system yet
Trainer chat / booking
No messaging or session scheduling
Stripe auto-pay
Payments today are tracked manually — cash / GCash
